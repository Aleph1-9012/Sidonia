# Limitations and release gates

- The packages require their documented exact framebuffer. There is no `auto` profile or runtime image scaling.
- A theme cannot rename real menu entries, inject fixture numeric prefixes, or choose the production default row.
- Decorative diagnostics are static artwork and do not report live telemetry.
- The 24-case BIOS/UEFI QEMU matrix and separate golden approval are release gates. Goldens are never regenerated by the comparison command.
- Real-hardware testing follows isolated QEMU success and is never performed first.
- T4 is gated by the stock-GRUB selector geometry spike documented in `themes/t4/compositions/selector-spike.md`.

## Current blocked fidelity gates

- T4 remains blocked. The candidate `160px` item / `-48px` spacing geometry has not passed the required BIOS and UEFI captures at all three modes and all four selector positions. Candidate T4 output exists only to support that isolated evaluation; it is not a completed T4 implementation.
- Construction previews currently place selector artwork but do not render live fixture titles or timeout state. They are not fidelity goldens.
- The four timeout regions still require reference-derived neutral backgrounds and fixed masks above the standard `__timeout__` fill. The current solid candidate styles do not preserve all ruler/cell gaps.
- T3's candidate selector canvas is offset from the authoritative network spine and its neutralization patch requires remeasurement to restore connector continuity.
- T1's candidate menu neutralization is a flat patch; exact idle texture, dividers, tick rail, and selector recut remain outstanding.
- Stock GRUB provides one title run per menu item. The separate number/title typography in T2 and T4 needs a proven stock-GRUB composition or an explicit engine-limitation decision; production prefixes may not be baked or injected.
- The digest-pinned OCI release container remains outstanding. The current host-locked build verifies platform, executable bytes, GRUB/QEMU/OVMF, Sharp/libvips, libpng, and spng exactly.
