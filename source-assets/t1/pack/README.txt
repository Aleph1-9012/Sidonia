T1-F INDUSTRIAL FIRMWARE UI ASSET PACK

Transparent raster UI components derived from the supplied T1-F reference.

Contents
--------
- selectors: selected/unselected rows and key selectors
- frames: rounded device/view frames and angular brackets
- icons: crossed-circle, caution, hazard, grille, and direction glyphs
- countdown: 7, 4, 704, 06, and T-06 graphics
- scrollbars: vertical tracks, pointers, rulers, and progress strip
- overlays: white/red angular plates and footer overlay
- micrographics: registration marks, dimensions, barcodes, hazard bands,
  nodes, circuit lines, signal ticks, and caution marker
- structural_panels: blank white/red plates, center bezel, and footer deck
- menu_blocks: complete labeled menu plus reusable blank menu variants
- footer_controls: full labeled/blank strips and individual controls
- status_labels: headers, boot ruler, serial/spec blocks, caution, and barcode
- brand_lockups: CJK/704 mark, stencil IDs, stripe badge, and identifier marks
- guide_overlays: full-screen registration, corner marks, rails, and dimensions
- texture_overlays: transparent scratch, print, scanline, and dust textures
- atlases: one transparent source sheet per category
- preview.png: checkerboard-backed visual index
- manifest.json: dimensions, paths, and SHA-256 checksums

All production assets are PNG RGBA with transparent exterior pixels. The pack
contains style-matched raster derivatives rather than pixel-for-pixel crops.
